How it works

You teach the topic your usual way. Then, on sim day, each team gets its company's event card, argues it out in rotating executive roles (CEO, CPO, COO, CFMO), and fills in a one-page decision form. You type their letters into the workbook, and the news feed reveals what happened: stock moves, morale shifts, sometimes a resignation.

Grades come from a weekly one-page decision memo, individual role briefs and a final shareholder meeting, never from the stock price. Scoring rewards improvement, so the struggling company can beat the market leader.

Questions

Do I need a specific textbook?

No. Teach each topic with your own book and lessons. The teacher guide includes a topic map with a column for your textbook's chapters.

How much time does it take each week?

About 10–15 minutes outside class to type each team's choices into the workbook. Everything else is print-and-go.

Do I need Excel?

The workbook has no macros and runs in desktop Excel or the free Excel for the web. Google Sheets isn't recommended.

My class isn't 24 students. Will it work?

Yes. The guide covers running 3–5 companies for smaller classes and teams of five for larger ones (12–32 students).
