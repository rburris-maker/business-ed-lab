About

Hi, I'm Ryan Burris

I'm a high school business teacher. I teach college-level business courses through a dual-credit CTE pathway, so my students earn college credit while they're still in high school. That covers management, marketing, accounting, economics, entrepreneurship and more.

I hold an MBA in Data Analytics, and I also advise a business student organization and coach high school soccer. Most of what I've learned about teaching comes from the same place: people learn by doing, then naming what they did.

That's the idea behind Business Ed Lab. Students meet the problem first and get the textbook term second. Every resource here was built for my own classroom and tested with my own students before it was offered to anyone else.

What I believe about business class

  • Students should make decisions, not just define terms
  • Good activities come back again and again, not once and done
  • Teachers deserve materials that are ready to run tomorrow
